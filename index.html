Observatories

Observatory locations

Where each selected instrument is sited. Instruments sharing an observatory are nudged apart slightly so they don’t overlap.

Site seeing conditions

Typical/median atmospheric seeing published for each site (site-testing literature) — not part of the original instrument dataset. Approximate and season/epoch-dependent — treat as indicative, not a live measurement. violet name/link = sourced online, click ↗ to view

Wavelength coverage

Bandwidth range per instrument, coloured by physical wavelength — the visible band (~380–750 nm) in true spectral colour, the near-infrared fading to grey since it lies beyond human vision.

Fiber / slit aperture on sky

On-sky size of each instrument's light-collecting aperture. Multiple values are discrete selectable configurations, not a continuous range — dashed grey rings mark future/in-development instruments.
True IFU/fiber-bundle spatial layouts aren’t in the current dataset, so multi-fiber facilities (KiwiSpec, PARVI) are shown as one representative aperture rather than their real fiber geometry.

Spectral resolution

Resolving power R = λ/Δλ. Each dot is a distinct operating mode — not a continuous range.

RV precision vs. resolution

Point size scales with telescope aperture. Precision axis is logarithmic (cm/s to km/s span four orders of magnitude).

Overall throughput (atmosphere + telescope + spectrograph)

Measured or estimated efficiency range. Full wavelength-dependent transmission curves are being requested from instrument teams and will replace these ranges as they arrive.
● Marked instruments report spectrograph-only throughput (atmosphere and telescope losses excluded), so are not directly comparable to the rest.

Spectral sampling

Detector sampling per resolution element, shown as a Gaussian FWHM footprint: width = dispersion-direction sampling (px), height = cross-dispersion sampling (px). Populated as instrument teams reply.
No sampling data yet — this fills in as instrument teams reply to the pixel-sampling question.
